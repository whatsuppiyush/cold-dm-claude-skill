# Account Safety — keep your X account healthy

Cold DMs work only if the account stays alive and the messages land in the inbox. X aggressively
flags accounts that behave like spam bots. This skill optimizes for **inbox placement and reply
rate**, not raw volume — you scale by adding accounts and sending better, not by hammering one account.

Read this before any sending or engagement run.

## What gets accounts flagged

- **Volume spikes.** Going from 0 to 50 DMs/day overnight. New accounts sending like established ones.
- **Bursts.** Firing a batch of DMs, follows, or likes back-to-back in minutes. Humans don't do that.
- **Low reply rate / high report rate.** Generic, irrelevant, salesy DMs get ignored, marked as spam,
  or reported — the single strongest ban signal. Targeting and personalization *are* safety features.
- **Links in cold first DMs.** Especially shortened links or the same URL to many strangers.
- **Identical copy** sent to many recipients (a literal template with no variation).
- **Follow/unfollow churn** and mass-follow sprees for reach.
- **A cold, empty account** (no avatar/bio/history) suddenly doing outreach.

## Conservative daily pacing

Treat these as ceilings, and ramp up to them — don't start here on day one:

| Account age / trust | DMs / day | Follows / day | Likes+replies / day |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| New / cold (< a few weeks, thin history) | 5–10 | 5–10 | 5–10 |
| Warming (few weeks, some activity) | 10–20 | 10–20 | 10–20 |
| Established (aged, real history, good standing) | 20–30 | 20–30 | 20–30 |

- **Spread across the day** with randomized gaps (jitter) — never a single burst. Sending 20 DMs
  should look like a person messaging through the day, not a script at 3am.
- **Ramp gradually.** Add a few more per day over a week or two rather than jumping to the cap.
- **Scale with accounts, not volume.** Need more reach? Add another connected account at a safe pace,
  don't push one account past its ceiling.
- **Weekends/odd hours** with mechanical regularity read as automation — keep it human.

## Warm up first

For higher-value prospects, engage before you DM:

1. **Follow** the prospect.
2. **Like** (and occasionally **reply** to) a recent, relevant post — something you'd genuinely
   endorse, with a real comment, not "great post!".
3. **Wait a day or two**, then send the DM. It now comes from a familiar name, not a stranger.

Also warm up the *account itself*: a real avatar, bio, a few genuine posts, and some organic
following/engagement before it ever sends a cold DM. An account that only sends DMs looks like exactly
what it is.

## The mindset

Every guardrail here also makes the outreach *work better*: tighter targeting → higher reply rate →
lower report rate → healthier account → more inbox placement. Safety and results point the same way.

**Refuse the opposite.** Mass-blasting an untargeted list, buying followers, rapid-fire follow/unfollow,
or pushing an account past these limits is spam — it gets accounts banned and it is not what this skill
does. If a user asks for it, decline and steer them back to a smaller, better-fit list sent at a human
pace.
